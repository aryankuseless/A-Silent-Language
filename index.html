The face & body

Kinesics

0facial muscles, making thousands of expressions.

The study of body movement, gestures and facial expression. Joy, sorrow, fear and anger are recognised in every culture on Earth, and steady eye contact builds authority while staring at the floor signals a lack of confidence.

  • Palms up: honesty & openness
  • Pointing: tension & authority
  • Eye contact: trust & confidence

Space & touch · Edward T. Hall

Proxemics

Four invisible bubbles, and one handshake that says it all.

Intimate is for family, personal for friends, social for business and public for talks like this one. Breach a bubble too early and the brain triggers a subtle threat response. Haptics, communication through touch, completes the picture.

  • Firm & parallel: mutual respect
  • Palm down: a bid for dominance
  • Limp: lack of energy

Time & the things we wear

Chronemics

Time is a currency of respect. Your attire is a cognitive shortcut.

Arriving early signals competence and commitment; arriving late says your time matters more than theirs. Clothes, accessories and grooming shape your credibility before your arguments are even heard.

  • 10 min early: competence & commitment
  • Late: “my time matters more”
  • Artifacts: clothes, grooming, accessories

Body language has no global dictionary

Culture

Non-verbal communication is shaped by culture. A gesture that means agreement or respect here can be a serious insult abroad.

Thumbs up

West: success
Parts of West Africa & Middle East: vulgar insult

The “OK” sign

Here: perfect
South America: derogatory

Eye contact

Here: confidence
Several East Asian cultures: challenge to authority
